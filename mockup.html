Household Expenses mockup

The question: is this simple, mobile-first layout the right shape for the app? It has five tabs: Home, Groceries, Bills, Rent and Furniture. Household settings and the bond sit behind the people icon on Home. It uses the same fake data and the same rules as wireframe.html. Nothing is saved.

Try

  • As Alice (primary), enter the Electricity bill from Home's To do.
  • Switch to Bob: the + buttons and admin actions disappear, and he can tick only his own shares.
  • Press +1 month: new rent and bills show up in To do.
  • As Cara, open Household and tap Leave household.
  • Switch to Dan and join with ELM-4821.
